Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

What the app accesses

When you choose "Sign in with Google", Van Press CRM asks for permission to:

  • Send email on your behalf (gmail.send) — only when you press "Send" in the app.
  • Read your email (gmail.readonly) — to show recent incoming messages and client replies inside the app.

How data is used and stored

  • The app runs entirely in your browser. It has no server and does not send your data to anyone except Google's Gmail API.
  • Emails you add to bookings are stored only in your own browser (local storage) on your device.
  • The Google access token is kept only in the open browser tab and is discarded when you reload or sign out.
  • The app never deletes or changes emails in your Gmail account.
  • Data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Van Press CRM's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

You can sign out in the app's settings at any time, or remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To delete data stored by the app, clear the site data in your browser.
